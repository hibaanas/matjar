About Lumora

A global store built for the way you actually shop

Lumora started as a small studio sourcing hand-picked pieces from independent designers, and grew into a home for fashion lovers across the globe. Every item on our shelves is chosen for quality, comfort, and character — never mass-produced filler.

We work directly with over 300 makers and factories, cutting out unnecessary middlemen so you get premium quality at honest prices, with checkout-to-doorstep delivery in days, not weeks.
